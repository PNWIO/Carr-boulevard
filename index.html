Project Overview

A Development Ready to Move

Carr Boulevard represents a rare opportunity in Kitsap County: a fully permitted, mixed-use residential development on a shovel-ready site. We've handled the entitlements. Now it's ready for builders who see the opportunity.

  • 32 residential townhome lots
  • 1 mixed-use commercial parcel
  • Professional open space and stormwater management
  • Six distinct, market-tested unit types
  • Modern landscape plan, native plantings, City of Bremerton approved
  • Fully entitled — all municipal reviews complete
  • Civil engineering by N.L. Olson & Associates

Location: Bremerton, Kitsap County, Washington — strong regional market, proximity to employment centers and urban amenities.

Six Unit Types Built for Market

Each unit type has been developed with end-user and builder economics in mind. Mix and match across the 38 units to optimize your development strategy.

Type A

3BR / 2.5BA

Entry-level townhome with efficient open plan. Ground-floor den. Two-car garage.

Type B

3BR / 2.5BA

Mid-market offering with upgraded finish package. Primary suite with walk-in closet. Patio space.
